About Handover Studio & How It Works

An intelligent, autonomous, multimodal AI workplace platform designed to capture how employees perform complex tasks and transform that knowledge into verified instructions anyone can follow.

The Platform Vision

"Do your work once. Let AI turn it into a verified handover anyone can follow."

Handover Studio bridges the gap between how employees actually perform complex digital work and the documentation required for someone else to step in seamlessly. Rather than forcing employees to manually type lengthy manuals or take hundreds of screenshots before annual leave or job transitions, Handover Studio uses autonomous multimodal AI agents to observe permitted screen interactions, sequence the workflow, surface undocumented knowledge gaps, generate five synchronous delivery formats, and verify colleague readiness.

The Tribal Knowledge Crisis

  • Exhausting Manual Documentation: Preparing handovers takes 10–20 hours of typing, taking screenshots, and recording video tutorials that no one reads.
  • Invisible Knowledge Gaps: Employees omit implicit decision rules (e.g. "if quota < 50, don't clean data"), causing errors when colleagues cover.
  • Rapid Document Decay: Static wiki pages become outdated the moment an application UI changes or an enterprise pipeline shifts.
  • Colleague Anxiety & Emergency Interruptions: Absent staff get interrupted on holiday because covering peers lack passwords, VPN paths, or clear escalation contacts.

The Handover Studio Solution

  • Demonstrate Naturally (Watch Me Work): Employees complete their task once on screen. Autonomous agents observe clicks, windows, and OCR context.
  • AI Gap Detection & Targeted Clarification: Autonomous Agent 4 detects implicit assumptions and prompts for answers in a clean questionnaire.
  • Single Source-of-Truth Multiformat Generation: Approved workflows automatically produce Word SOPs (.docx), PowerPoint decks (.pptx), captioned videos (.vtt), and interactive vector flowcharts (.svg).
  • Separation of Reusable SOPs & Coverage Packs: Timeless standard operating procedures are stored in the Process Library, while holiday handovers are organized into time-specific Coverage Packs with pre-flight access checks.

The 10-Agent Autonomous Pipeline Architecture

Handover Studio does not rely on a single generic chatbot. It orchestrates 10 specialized, modular AI agents that operate across five sequential execution stages.

Stage 1: Capture Agent 1
Observation & Event Analyzer

Parses recorded video frames, user clicks, active applications, and OCR text. Filters idle noise and identifies candidate workflow events.

Stage 2: Sequence Agent 2
Procedural Workflow Sequencer

Structures raw interactions into a chronological sequence of meaningful milestones, establishing clear start, middle, and completion states.

Stage 2: Synthesize Agent 3
Step Synthesizer & Rule Formulator

Generates actionable step titles, detailed execution instructions, decision thresholds, and explicit verification checks.

Stage 3: Gap Detection Agent 4
Knowledge Gap Hunter

Scans the reconstructed workflow for implicit assumptions, missing prerequisites, or undocumented branching rules that were not visible on screen.

Stage 3: Clarification Agent 5
Clarification Interviewer

Formulates concise, non-leading questions for the employee, then incorporates their answers directly into the workflow model.

Stage 4: Documentation Agent 6
Documentation Author (SOP)

Generates comprehensive standard operating procedures formatted for enterprise distribution, exportable as Microsoft Word (.docx).

Stage 4: Visuals Agent 7
Process Map & Flowchart Architect

Renders interactive, zoomable vector flowcharts (.svg) with color-coded nodes for actions, decisions, and verification gates.

Stage 4: Video Agent 8
Video Production Engineer

Packages the demonstration video with synchronized chapter markers and downloadable WebVTT caption subtitles.

Stage 4: Slides Agent 9
Executive Presentation Deck Builder

Compiles clean, branded executive slide presentations exportable directly as PowerPoint (.pptx).

Stage 5: Verification Agent 10
Readiness Auditor & Practice Coach

Evaluates colleague walkthrough sessions, logs friction points, computes objective readiness scores, and recommends workflow improvements.

Interactive Intelligence Zero-Hallucination Engine
Grounded AI Colleague Assistant

Empowers covering colleagues to ask conversational questions about the task (e.g. "What do I do if responses fall under 50?"). The engine enforces strict citation grounding: it cites the exact procedural step and refuses to hallucinate information not demonstrated or confirmed by the owner.

How to Use Handover Studio: Practical Workflows

Follow these three practical workflows to create, review, and execute workplace handovers with zero friction.

Workflow A

Recording a Task & Publishing an SOP

1
Launch Recording in "Watch Me Work"
Click Watch Me Work in the sidebar. Select your screen or window, then click Start Recording. Perform your usual routine in your business tools (e.g. Qualtrics, Excel, Jira, AWS, internal platforms). Use the Add Marker button at key milestones.
2
Trigger AI Agent Workflow Reconstruction
Click Stop Recording, review your playback, and click Run AI Agent Workflow Reconstruction. Agents 1–4 will automatically sequence actions into procedural steps and detect potential knowledge gaps.
3
Review in Review Studio & Answer Knowledge Gaps
Inspect the reconstructed workflow on the right and video playback on the left. Answer any clarification questions flagged by Agent 4 (e.g. thresholds or passwords). Edit any step instructions or click Redact Confidential Data to mask passwords and client PII.
4
Approve & Generate Multi-Format Materials
Click Approve & Publish Handover. The system locks the single-source-of-truth model and synchronizes it into Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), captioned video (.vtt), and flowchart (.svg). It is now published in your Process Library!
Workflow B

Creating a Holiday Coverage Pack

1
Launch Guided Wizard & Choose Coverage Dates
Open Coverage Packs and click Create Holiday Coverage Pack. Enter your handover title, select your leave dates (e.g. Nov 3 – Nov 17), and designate your covering colleague (e.g. Alex Rivera).
2
Assign Recurring Duties & Link Approved SOPs
Add responsibilities (e.g. "Export Weekly CSAT Responses"), set due times (e.g. Friday 14:00 UTC), and link them directly to verified instructions from your Process Library.
3
Provide Written Context & Emergency Escalation Contacts
Add operational context that cannot be captured in a screen recording: active client SLAs, current project volume, decision rules, and emergency WhatsApp/Slack escalation triggers.
4
Publish & Hand Off
Review the summary in Step 4 and click Publish Coverage Pack. The pack goes live immediately in the colleague's dedicated workspace.
Workflow C

Executing Tasks as a Covering Colleague

1
Review Pre-Flight Systems Access Checklist
Open My Assigned Tasks. Check the green verified access badges for Qualtrics XM, Enterprise Data Hub, and Slack alerts. Test your SSO credentials before the owner leaves on vacation.
2
Follow Step-by-Step SOP Instructions
For each assigned task, click 📖 Open Step-by-Step SOP to view the exact sequence, decision rules, cautions, and validation checks.
3
Toggle Completion or Flag Operational Blockers
When finished, click the task checkbox to log completion. If an unexpected hurdle occurs (e.g. SSO locked, VPN failure), click ⚠️ Flag Blocker to record notes and immediately alert the leadership escalation channel.

Security, Governance & Frequently Asked Questions

Enterprise-grade privacy protections, consent safeguards, and answers to common technical and operational questions.

Is screen recording safe, transparent, and consent-driven?

Yes, completely. Handover Studio strictly adheres to an explicit consent architecture. There is zero background, silent, or continuous employee surveillance. Recording is initiated manually by the employee via the "Watch Me Work" button, indicated by prominent flashing REC badges and on-screen timers, and can be paused or discarded at any time.

How does Handover Studio handle confidential passwords and customer PII?

The platform features a multi-modal redaction engine. Users can highlight sensitive text, tokens, or customer names in the Review Studio or click "Redact Confidential Data". The redaction engine applies irreversible black-box masking across video frames, OCR extraction text, and all generated documents (.docx, .pptx, flowcharts).

Can the AI assistant invent or hallucinate instructions?

No. The Grounded AI Colleague Assistant is governed by strict citation grounding. Every claim made in an answer must cite the specific demonstrated step and timestamp. If a colleague asks about an edge case or procedure that was neither observed in the video nor answered by the owner, the assistant is programmatically constrained to declare: "This information was not demonstrated in the approved workflow."

Why are the Process Library and Coverage Packs kept separate?

Standard operating procedures (SOPs) represent reusable, enduring organizational knowledge that should remain active indefinitely. In contrast, Coverage Packs are time-specific operational transfers (e.g. Sarah taking annual leave from Nov 3 to Nov 17). Decoupling them allows one approved SOP (e.g. Weekly CSAT Tracker Export) to be reused across dozens of vacation transfers, role handovers, or new employee onboardings without duplicating documentation.

How is the 0–100% Handover Readiness Score calculated?

Agent 10 calculates the readiness score based on objective procedural criteria: percentage of steps with verified evidence, complete decision rules, explicit validation checks, zero unanswered knowledge gaps, and successful test walkthrough completion by covering peers. Handover Studio never fabricates readiness percentages.
